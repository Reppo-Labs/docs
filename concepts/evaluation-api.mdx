The **Reppo Evaluation API** checks your agent's work before it ships. Send a claim, an AI response, or an agent action, and you get back a verdict: a score you can gate on, how confident the result is, a written reason, and a route your pipeline can act on.

Use it from the console at [eval.reppo.xyz](https://eval.reppo.xyz), or point your agent at the API. New accounts start with 22,000 credits, enough for 50 text-only evaluations.

## Why teams use it

**Shrink the review pile.** Instead of a person reviewing every output, they review the small share that comes back flagged. That's the difference between an agent that needs a babysitter and one that runs on its own.

**Prove the work was checked.** Every verdict records what was judged, how confident the result is, why it was routed the way it was, and each evaluator's scores and reasoning. For teams working under rules like the EU AI Act, that record is what you hand an auditor.

**Make your agents better.** The stream of verdicts is a reward signal. Feed it into preference pairs or inference-time selection, and your agent improves on real production data instead of stale labels.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Send your submission with context">
    Submit from the console or the API. Choose the type of submission (`claim`, `response`, or `action`), and include the context the judges need in the text itself: for a response, the user's prompt too; for an action, what the user asked for. You can attach one image or PDF.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A panel of evaluators scores it">
    The evaluation is queued and scored in the background by a panel of independent evaluators, usually within 30 to 90 seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reppo returns one verdict">
    The evaluators' scores are combined into one verdict: a score from 0 to 100, a decision, a confidence level, and a written reason.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Route on the decision">
    Ship goes ahead, review goes to a person, block stops it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you get back

| Part | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Score | 0 to 100 overall quality |
| Decision | `accept` or `reject`, at the policy threshold |
| Confidence | `high`, `medium`, or `low`: how much the evaluators agreed. Thin or ambiguous submissions still get a verdict, with lower confidence. |
| Reason | A one-paragraph explanation |
| Rubric scores | The per-dimension breakdown |
| Route | `ship`, `review`, or `block` |

You can use the route directly, or set your own threshold from the score and confidence.

| Route | What to do |
|---|---|
| `ship` | Ship it |
| `review` | Send it back, or to a person |
| `block` | Block it and log it |

## Getting better verdicts

The API takes a `taskType` and an `input`, with no separate context or criteria fields. Put what the judges need to check the work inside `input` itself: for a response, the user's prompt and the answer; for an action, what the user asked for and what the agent did. If you attach an image or PDF, say in `input` what to assess about it.

## How fast results come back

From the console, you can get a verdict right away.

Evaluations are scored in the background, usually within 30 to 90 seconds. Poll the result until it is `COMPLETED` or `FAILED`.

## Pricing

Prepaid credits, with no subscription.

- New accounts start with 22,000 credits, a one-time sign-up bonus.
- A text-only evaluation costs 440 credits. One with an image or PDF attached costs 1,000 credits.
- A person buys more credits on the Billing page at [eval.reppo.xyz/billing](https://eval.reppo.xyz/billing).

You're only charged once the evaluation is `COMPLETED`. If an evaluation fails, it's free.

## Who can see your submission

<Warning>
  Evaluations are public for now: other signed-in users can see submissions on the network feed at [eval.reppo.xyz/evals](https://eval.reppo.xyz/evals). Don't submit confidential material.
</Warning>

The feed never shows who submitted an evaluation.

## Evaluate with your agent

Point any agent at [eval.reppo.xyz/agents.md](https://eval.reppo.xyz/agents.md). From there it can submit its own work for evaluation.
